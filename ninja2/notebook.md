### The author's own patch predates the spec

One survey specimen, dated 2004-09-27, is a translation by the format's author two years before the 2006 filespec. It carries a 2045-byte preamble — its description field three bytes short of the later 1074 — six files, and an embedded NUL in the description. It is the one specimen no reader handles, and the only multi-file one. The 2048-byte canonical preamble reads like a later rounding to a tidy sector; this is what came just before it.
